Dear Partners and Stakeholders,

I am writing to formally introduce and seek your collaboration on **PepsiCo's Transborder Shipment Programme**, which establishes structured operational corridors for moving PepsiCo goods from **Sohar Port (Oman)**, **Khorfakkan Port (UAE)**, and **Fujairah Port (UAE)** into the **Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA)**.

---

### Purpose of the Initiative

As part of PepsiCo's ongoing commitment to supply chain excellence in the Middle East, we are implementing a standardised, multi-corridor approach to transborder logistics. This initiative will:

- Provide **supply continuity** and route redundancy into Saudi Arabia
- Establish **clear operational procedures** and documentation standards across all three port corridors
- Achieve **consistent customs compliance** with Saudi, UAE, and Omani regulatory requirements
- Deliver **cost-efficient logistics solutions** that support PepsiCo's commercial growth in KSA

Saudi Arabia represents one of PepsiCo's most important markets in the region, and ensuring reliable, uninterrupted product availability is a strategic priority.

---

### Key Benefits

For all stakeholders involved in this programme, the key benefits include:

| Stakeholder Group | Key Benefit |
|-------------------|-------------|
| **PepsiCo** | Supply chain resilience, lower per-unit logistics cost, improved lead-time predictability |
| **Carriers & Freight Forwarders** | Long-term frame agreements, predictable volume commitments, streamlined documentation |
| **Port Authorities** | Increased throughput volumes, long-term commercial partnership with a global FMCG brand |
| **Customs Authorities** | Pre-submitted declarations, fully compliant documentation, reduced customs inspection time |
| **3PL & Road Transport Providers** | Consistent freight volumes, clear operating procedures, milestone-based KPI framework |
| **Customs Brokers** | Predictable workflow, frame agreements, clear escalation protocols |

### Required Actions from Each Stakeholder

We kindly request the following actions from each party by the indicated dates:

**Carrier Partners (Shipping Lines):**
- Confirm vessel schedule and capacity allocation for the Sohar, Khorfakkan, and Fujairah corridors
- Provide commercial rate proposals for FCL/LCL services on applicable trade lanes
- Confirm process for Telex Release / Sea Waybill issuance for KSA-bound shipments
- **Action deadline:** 2 weeks from receipt of this letter

**Port Authorities (Sohar, Khorfakkan, Fujairah):**
- Confirm terminal handling charges (THC), free storage allowance, and extended storage rates
- Provide port operational contacts for pre-arrival notification (PAN) submissions
- Share any port-specific requirements or restrictions for FMCG / food-grade cargo
- **Action deadline:** 2 weeks from receipt of this letter

**Customs Authorities (ZATCA — Saudi Arabia):**
- Confirm current import documentation requirements for food and beverage products
- Advise on pre-clearance capabilities via Fasah for regular PepsiCo shipment lanes
- Provide guidance on SFDA and SASO inspection procedures at land border crossings
- **Action deadline:** 3 weeks from receipt of this letter

**Customs Authorities (UAE — Sharjah / Fujairah):**
- Confirm export and transit declaration requirements for goods transiting to KSA
- Advise on Free Zone re-export procedures from Sharjah FZ and Fujairah FZ
- **Action deadline:** 3 weeks from receipt of this letter

**Customs Authorities (Oman):**
- Confirm transit bond/guarantee requirements for goods entering KSA via the Batha crossing
- Provide updated procedure for Bayan system transit declarations
- **Action deadline:** 3 weeks from receipt of this letter

**Customs Brokers (KSA, UAE, Oman):**
- Review and acknowledge the SOP (PEP-LOG-SOP-001) and document checklist
- Confirm operational capability and team availability to support programme go-live
- Sign the PepsiCo broker service level agreement (SLA) and confidentiality agreement
- **Action deadline:** 2 weeks from receipt of this letter

**Third-Party Logistics (3PL) and Road Transport Providers:**
- Confirm fleet availability and capacity for port-to-KSA routes
- Provide rate schedules for all three port corridors (Sohar, Khorfakkan, Fujairah) to Dammam, Riyadh, and Jeddah
- Confirm GPS tracking capability and willingness to integrate with PepsiCo TMS
- **Action deadline:** 2 weeks from receipt of this letter
