# Step-by-Step Operational Workflow
## PepsiCo Transborder Shipments — Port to KSA Warehouse

**Document No:** PEP-LOG-WF-001 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** March 2026

---

## Overview

This workflow maps the end-to-end operational sequence for moving PepsiCo shipments from Sohar Port (Oman), Khorfakkan Port (UAE), or Fujairah Port (UAE) to PepsiCo's warehouses or distributors within Saudi Arabia. Each phase builds on the previous and includes parallel documentation activities.

---

## Phase 1 — Pre-Shipment & Booking

```
[1.1] PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager receives confirmed Purchase Orders (POs) from KSA Demand Planning
       ↓
[1.2] Determine optimal port corridor based on:
       • Vessel availability and schedule
       • Destination in KSA (Eastern, Central, or Western region)
       • Current port congestion status
       • Cost comparison (Sohar vs. Khorfakkan vs. Fujairah)
       ↓
[1.3] Freight Forwarder is instructed and vessel booking is confirmed
       → Booking Reference Number (BRN) issued
       ↓
[1.4] Compliance checks initiated (PARALLEL with booking):
       • Confirm Halal certificate validity
       • Confirm SFDA product registration is current
       • Confirm SASO conformity certificate is valid
       • Request Certificate of Origin from Chamber of Commerce
```

---

## Phase 2 — Documentation Preparation

```
[2.1] Shipper/Exporter prepares and submits to Freight Forwarder:
       • Commercial Invoice (3 originals)
       • Packing List
       • Certificate of Origin
       • Halal Certificate
       • SFDA Registration certificate
       • SASO Conformity Certificate
       • Phytosanitary / Health Certificate (if applicable)
       • Fumigation Certificate (if timber packing used)
       ↓
[2.2] Freight Forwarder reviews documents for accuracy and completeness
       → If deficiency found: notify PepsiCo immediately; request correction
       ↓
[2.3] Export Declaration filed at origin customs:
       • Sohar route  → Oman Customs (Bayan system)
       • Khorfakkan   → Sharjah Customs portal
       • Fujairah     → Fujairah Customs portal
       ↓
[2.4] KSA Customs Broker pre-submits import declaration on ZATCA Fasah platform
       → Draft declaration lodged; finalised upon BL issuance
```

---

## Phase 3 — Port Receiving & Loading Operations

```
[3.1] Cargo arrives at port / container freight station (CFS)
       ↓
[3.2] Port Agent confirms container arrival and validates:
       • Container number matches booking
       • Seal number matches shipping instructions
       • Weight within port limits
       ↓
[3.3] VGM (Verified Gross Mass) declaration submitted to port
       ↓
[3.4] Container loaded onto nominated vessel
       → Mate's Receipt issued
       ↓
[3.5] Bill of Lading (BL) or Sea Waybill issued by Shipping Line
       → Original BL / Telex Release transmitted to Consignee / Customs Broker
       ↓
[3.6] Freight Forwarder sends full document set to KSA Customs Broker:
       • BL copy, Invoice, Packing List, CoO, Halal, SFDA, SASO certs
```

---

## Phase 4 — Ocean Transit

```
[4.1] Vessel departs origin port (or connects via feeder/transhipment if applicable)
       ↓
[4.2] Freight Forwarder tracks vessel via carrier portal / vessel tracking system
       ↓
[4.3] Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) at discharge port confirmed and shared with:
       • Port Agent (Sohar / Khorfakkan / Fujairah)
       • KSA Customs Broker
       • PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager
       ↓
[4.4] KSA Customs Broker finalises Fasah import declaration:
       → Declaration submitted and awaiting customs channel assignment
         (Green channel = auto-release | Yellow = document review | Red = physical inspection)
```

---

## Phase 5 — Port Discharge Operations

```
[5.1] Port Agent files Pre-Arrival Notification (PAN) at least 48 hours before vessel arrival
       ↓
[5.2] Vessel arrives and berths at Sohar / Khorfakkan / Fujairah
       ↓
[5.3] Containers discharged and placed in Container Yard (CY)
       ↓
[5.4] Port Customs performs risk-based scanning / inspection (if triggered):
       → GREEN: Container released immediately
       → YELLOW: Document review required (typically 4–12 hours)
       → RED: Physical examination (24–48 hours)
       ↓
[5.5] Customs Broker (UAE/Oman) pays port charges:
       • Terminal Handling Charges (THC)
       • Storage fees (if free days exceeded)
       ↓
[5.6] Delivery Order (DO) issued to nominated Road Transport Provider
       → Container available for pickup from CY
```

---

## Phase 6 — Road Transport to KSA Border

```
[6.1] Road Transport Provider assigns truck and driver
       → Container collected from port CY
       ↓
[6.2] Driver briefed on:
       • Route to border crossing (Batha or Hili)
       • Documents to carry (Invoice, Packing List, CoO, BL copy, Customs release)
       • Contact number for PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager
       ↓
[6.3] Transit manifest submitted at origin customs (UAE exit / Oman exit)
       → Exit stamp affixed on transit documents
       ↓
[6.4] PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager notified:
       → "Container [CNTR No.] departed [Port Name] at [Time] — ETA border [Date/Time]"
       ↓
[6.5] Truck travels to KSA border crossing:
       • Sohar → Batha border crossing (Oman–KSA)
       • Khorfakkan / Fujairah → Hili or Al Batha (UAE–KSA)
```

---

## Phase 7 — KSA Border Customs Clearance

```
[7.1] Truck arrives at Saudi Arabia border crossing
       ↓
[7.2] KSA Customs Broker confirms import declaration is active on Fasah
       → Declaration includes all duties, taxes, and VAT calculations
       ↓
[7.3] Truck driver presents full document set to Saudi Customs officer:
       • Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading copy
       • Certificate of Origin, Halal Certificate, SFDA Certificate, SASO Certificate
       • Fasah customs declaration reference number
       ↓
[7.4] ZATCA customs officer verifies documents and reviews Fasah declaration
       → Channel assignment:
         GREEN  → Auto-release: proceed immediately
         YELLOW → Documentary review: 4–24 hours
         RED    → Physical inspection: 24–48 hours
       ↓
[7.5] SFDA / SASO officer may conduct product inspection at border (food/beverage category)
       ↓
[7.6] Import duties and VAT paid via Fasah portal by Customs Broker
       ↓
[7.7] Saudi Customs releases container:
       → Official clearance stamp affixed on delivery note
       → Truck permitted to enter KSA
       ↓
[7.8] PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager notified:
       → "Shipment cleared KSA customs at [Border] — ETA warehouse [Date/Time]"
```

---

## Phase 8 — Shipment Tracking & Visibility

```
[Ongoing throughout all phases]

[8.1] Freight Forwarder provides weekly shipment status reports to PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager
       ↓
[8.2] Port Agent provides real-time discharge and DO issuance updates
       ↓
[8.3] Road Transport Provider shares GPS/live truck location via transport management portal
       → PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager has access to live tracking dashboard
       ↓
[8.4] Milestone alerts sent to PepsiCo team at:
       • Vessel departure confirmation
       • ETA update (updated every 24 hours)
       • Container discharged at port
       • Container departed port gate
       • Truck arrived at KSA border
       • Customs cleared at KSA border
       • Truck arrived at PepsiCo warehouse
       ↓
[8.5] All tracking data recorded in PepsiCo TMS (Transport Management System)
       → Used for KPI measurement and quarterly performance review
```

---

## Phase 9 — Delivery to PepsiCo Warehouse or Distributor

```
[9.1] Truck arrives at designated PepsiCo KSA warehouse or distributor facility
       ↓
[9.2] Warehouse team conducts Goods Receipt (GR) inspection:
       • Count units/cartons against Packing List — report any shortages
       • Inspect for physical damage — photograph any damaged cargo
       • Check product expiry dates — reject any near-expiry stock per SOP
       • Verify temperature log for temperature-sensitive products (reefer)
       ↓
[9.3] Goods Receipt confirmed in PepsiCo ERP (SAP or equivalent)
       → Inventory updated; POs matched and closed
       ↓
[9.4] Proof of Delivery (POD) signed by warehouse supervisor
       → POD sent to:
         • Freight Forwarder (for carrier file closure)
         • PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager (KPI records)
         • Finance team (invoice matching and payment)
       ↓
[9.5] If discrepancies found:
       → Complete Discrepancy Report (Form: PEP-LOG-DR-001) within 24 hours
       → Notify Freight Forwarder and insurer if damage exceeds USD 1,000
       ↓
[9.6] Shipment file closed:
       → All documents archived (minimum 5 years per ZATCA requirements)
       → KPI data recorded for monthly performance review
```

## Workflow Summary — Key Milestones

| Milestone | Responsible Party | Target Timeline |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|
| Booking confirmed | Freight Forwarder | T-25 days |
| All documents ready | Exporter / Compliance Team | T-7 days |
| Export declaration filed | Local Customs Broker | T-3 days |
| Vessel departure | Shipping Line | Day 0 |
| Vessel arrival at discharge port | Shipping Line | Day 10–18 |
| Container discharged | Port Agent | Within 24h of berthing |
| Delivery Order issued | Port Agent | Within 24h of discharge |
| Truck departs port | Road Transport Provider | Within 24h of DO |
| Truck arrives KSA border | Road Transport Provider | Day 1 after port departure |
| KSA customs clearance complete | KSA Customs Broker | Within 48h of border arrival |
| Delivery to PepsiCo warehouse | Road Transport Provider | Day 2–3 after border entry |

---

> **Note:** All timelines are target benchmarks. Actual times depend on customs channel assignment, inspection requirements, and carrier performance. Exceptions are managed per the Exception Handling procedures in SOP PEP-LOG-SOP-001.
