# Summary: PepsiCo Transborder Shipment Movement into Saudi Arabia
## Sohar Port (Oman) | Khorfakkan Port (UAE) | Fujairah Port (UAE)

**Document No:** PEP-LOG-SUM-001 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** March 2026

---

## Key Points at a Glance

- **Objective:** Establish a reliable, compliant, and cost-efficient transborder shipping framework for PepsiCo goods entering Saudi Arabia (KSA) via three strategic ports — Sohar (Oman), Khorfakkan (UAE), and Fujairah (UAE).

- **Multi-Port Strategy:** A three-corridor approach is recommended — Sohar as the primary gateway for eastern KSA (Dammam, Al Khobar), Khorfakkan for high-frequency vessel services and central KSA, and Fujairah as a flex/overflow route with free-zone storage advantages.

- **Transit Times:** Total door-to-KSA-warehouse transit times range from approximately **18 to 29 days** depending on port, destination, and customs processing speed. Road legs (port to KSA border to warehouse) add an estimated 1–2 days for border clearance plus 6–24 hours driving time to final destination.

- **Customs Compliance — KSA:** All shipments must be cleared through ZATCA via the **Fasah** single-window platform. Key requirements include: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Halal Certificate, SFDA product registration, and SASO conformity certificate. Standard import duty is 5% (0% for GCC-origin goods under the GCC Customs Union).

- **Customs Compliance — Origin Countries:** Export declarations are filed through **Bayan** (Oman), **Sharjah Customs portal** (Khorfakkan), or **Fujairah Customs portal** (Fujairah). Transit bonds or guarantees are required for goods in transit through Oman and UAE.

- **Cost Range:** Road freight from port to KSA destination ranges from USD 800–1,850 per 40' container depending on port and destination. Port terminal handling charges (THC) are estimated at USD 120–200 per TEU. Additional costs include customs brokerage (USD 150–300 per shipment) and SASO/SFDA fees.

- **Top Risks Identified:** Customs documentation deficiencies causing holds at the KSA border are the highest-impact risk. Mitigation includes pre-clearance on Fasah, use of approved customs brokers, and thorough document validation before shipment departure.

- **SOP Framework:** A structured SOP (PEP-LOG-SOP-001) has been established covering eight process stages from shipment planning through delivery, with defined responsibilities for PepsiCo, freight forwarders, customs brokers, port agents, and road carriers.

- **Key Performance Indicators:** Core KPIs include ≥95% on-time port discharge, ≤48-hour border customs clearance, ≥98% documentation accuracy, ≥92% on-time delivery to warehouse, and ≤2% customs hold rate.

- **Safety Stock Recommendation:** PepsiCo KSA distribution centres should maintain a minimum of **3–4 weeks of safety stock** to buffer against potential border delays, shipping disruptions, or port congestion.

- **Feasibility Conclusion:** All three port corridors are operationally feasible for PepsiCo's transborder requirements. The combined use of Sohar, Khorfakkan, and Fujairah provides supply chain resilience, competitive cost options, and route redundancy. Implementation is recommended following stakeholder alignment and carrier/broker contracting.

- **Next Steps:** (1) Confirm carrier and customs broker frame agreements; (2) Validate cost assumptions with live quotations; (3) Conduct a pilot shipment on each corridor; (4) Integrate tracking into PepsiCo TMS; (5) Circulate SOP for team training and sign-off.
