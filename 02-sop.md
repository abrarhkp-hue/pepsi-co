# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
## Transborder Shipment Movement into Saudi Arabia
### Via Sohar Port (Oman), Khorfakkan Port (UAE), and Fujairah Port (UAE)

## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes a consistent, repeatable process for managing the transborder movement of PepsiCo shipments from Sohar Port (Oman), Khorfakkan Port (UAE), and Fujairah Port (UAE) into the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA). The SOP ensures:

- Compliance with applicable customs regulations in Oman, UAE, and Saudi Arabia
- Operational efficiency and minimum transit delays
- Clear accountability across all logistics stakeholders
- Standardised documentation and reporting

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:

- **Products:** All PepsiCo goods (beverages, snacks, and related FMCG products) imported into Saudi Arabia via the stated ports
- **Shipment types:** Full Container Load (FCL) and Less than Container Load (LCL)
- **Ports covered:** Sohar (Oman), Khorfakkan (UAE), Fujairah (UAE)
- **Border crossings covered:** Batha / Hili / Al Batha (Oman–KSA and UAE–KSA crossings)
- **Teams covered:** PepsiCo Supply Chain, Procurement, Customs Compliance, Third-party Logistics (3PL) providers, Freight Forwarders, Customs Brokers, Port Agents

---

## 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| **PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager** | Overall shipment planning, purchase order management, 3PL oversight |
| **PepsiCo Customs Compliance Team** | Ensure all shipments meet ZATCA, SASO, SFDA requirements; manage CoO and Halal certs |
| **Freight Forwarder** | Booking vessel space, coordinating port operations, issuing HBL, managing documentation |
| **Customs Broker (KSA)** | Filing Fasah declarations, liaising with ZATCA, managing duties and taxes |
| **Customs Broker (Oman/UAE)** | Export declarations, transit bonds, local port agent activities |
| **Port Agent (Sohar/Khorfakkan/Fujairah)** | Berth coordination, discharge oversight, delivery order issuance |
| **Road Transport Provider** | Truck allocation, border crossing, delivery to KSA warehouse/distributor |
| **PepsiCo KSA Warehouse** | Receiving, quality check, inventory update |

## 4. Process Steps

### Step 1 — Shipment Planning (T-30 to T-15 days before vessel departure)

1.1 PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager receives confirmed purchase orders (POs) from KSA demand planning team.  
1.2 Freight Forwarder is nominated based on the relevant port corridor (Sohar, Khorfakkan, or Fujairah).  
1.3 Vessel booking is confirmed; Booking Reference Number (BRN) is shared with all parties.  
1.4 Customs Compliance Team initiates product registration check with SFDA and Halal certificate validity check.  
1.5 Certificate of Origin (CoO) is requested from the origin country's chamber of commerce.

### Step 2 — Pre-Shipment Documentation (T-10 to T-5 days before vessel departure)

2.1 Freight Forwarder collects and reviews the following documents from shipper:

| # | Document | Issued by |
|---|----------|-----------|
| 1 | Commercial Invoice | Shipper/Exporter |
| 2 | Packing List | Shipper/Exporter |
| 3 | Certificate of Origin | Chamber of Commerce |
| 4 | Halal Certificate (food/beverage) | Accredited Halal Body |
| 5 | SASO/SFDA pre-approval or registration certificate | SASO/SFDA |
| 6 | Bill of Lading (MBL/HBL) | Shipping Line / Forwarder |
| 7 | Phytosanitary/Health Certificate (if applicable) | Competent Authority |
| 8 | Packing Declaration (Timber/Fumigation, if applicable) | Approved fumigator |

2.2 Freight Forwarder submits export declaration to:

- **Sohar corridor:** Oman Customs (Bayan system)
- **Khorfakkan corridor:** Sharjah Customs portal
- **Fujairah corridor:** Fujairah Customs portal

2.3 KSA Customs Broker prepares and pre-submits import declaration on Fasah platform using available documents.

### Step 3 — Port Operations at Origin/Transit Port

3.1 Freight Forwarder coordinates cargo stuffing, container sealing, and VGM (Verified Gross Mass) declaration.  
3.2 Port Agent confirms container arrival at port terminal and validates container number/seal against shipping documents.  
3.3 Container is loaded on nominated vessel; Mate's Receipt and then Bill of Lading are issued.  
3.4 Original Bill of Lading (or Sea Waybill/Telex Release, as agreed) is transmitted to the Consignee (PepsiCo KSA entity or Customs Broker).

### Step 4 — Port Discharge at Sohar/Khorfakkan/Fujairah

4.1 Port Agent is notified of vessel ETA (at least 48 hours in advance).  
4.2 Port Agent files Pre-Arrival Notification (PAN) with the port authority.  
4.3 Upon vessel arrival, containers are discharged and placed in the container yard (CY).  
4.4 Customs Broker (UAE/Oman) files port release request and pays applicable port charges (THC, storage if any).  
4.5 Delivery Order (DO) is issued to the nominated road transport provider.  
4.6 Cargo scanning/inspection by port customs is completed (if triggered by the system risk engine).

### Step 5 — Road Transport to KSA Border

5.1 Road transport provider collects the container from the port CY upon receipt of DO.  
5.2 Driver is briefed with route instructions and border crossing protocol.  
5.3 All original documents (Commercial Invoice, Packing List, CoO, Halal, BL copy) are carried with the truck.  
5.4 Transit manifest is submitted at the origin country customs (Oman/UAE) for border exit.  
5.5 PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager is notified when container departs port.

### Step 6 — KSA Border Customs Clearance

6.1 KSA Customs Broker submits final import declaration on **Fasah** prior to truck arrival at border.  
6.2 Truck driver presents documents to Saudi Customs officer at border crossing (Batha or Hili).  
6.3 ZATCA customs officer verifies declaration, documents, and may request physical inspection.  
6.4 Import duties and VAT (5% standard rate, unless exempt) are paid through the Fasah portal by the Customs Broker.  
6.5 Upon release, Saudi Customs affixes official clearance stamp on the delivery note.  
6.6 Truck is permitted to proceed into KSA.

### Step 7 — Delivery to PepsiCo KSA Warehouse / Distributor

7.1 Truck proceeds to the designated PepsiCo KSA warehouse or distributor per the routing instruction.  
7.2 Warehouse team conducts **goods receipt (GR)** inspection:

- Verify quantity against Packing List
- Check product condition (no damage, no leakage)
- Verify temperature/storage conditions (for applicable products)
- Check expiry dates

7.3 Goods Receipt is confirmed in PepsiCo's ERP system (SAP or equivalent).  
7.4 Proof of Delivery (POD) is signed and sent to Freight Forwarder and Supply Chain Manager.  
7.5 Any discrepancies are reported within 24 hours via the Discrepancy Report form (Form: PEP-LOG-DR-001).

## 6. Customs Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Location | System | Action |
|------------|----------|--------|--------|
| Export Declaration | Sohar / Khorfakkan / Fujairah | Bayan / Sharjah Customs / Fujairah Customs | Submitted by local Customs Broker prior to vessel loading |
| Port Security Scan | Port Terminal | Port authority scanner | Automated risk-based selection |
| Transit Declaration | UAE–KSA or Oman–KSA border (exit) | GCC Unified Customs system | Submitted by Freight Forwarder |
| KSA Import Declaration | Batha / Hili / Al Batha border (entry) | Fasah (ZATCA) | Filed by KSA Customs Broker; duties paid |
| SFDA / SASO Inspection | Border or designated inland port | SFDA/SASO officer | Risk-based product inspection; certification review |
| Final Customs Release | KSA border crossing | Fasah | Clearance stamp issued; truck released |

## 7. Exception Handling

### 7.1 Document Deficiency

- **Action:** Freight Forwarder notifies PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager immediately upon identifying missing document.
- **Resolution timeline:** Maximum 24 hours to supply missing document; if not resolved, shipment is placed on hold.
- **Escalation:** PepsiCo Customs Compliance Team and Legal are notified after 24 hours.

### 7.2 Customs Hold at KSA Border

- **Action:** KSA Customs Broker notifies PepsiCo within 2 hours of receiving hold notice.
- **Resolution:** Broker provides written explanation; PepsiCo team supports with additional documentation or samples if required.
- **Escalation:** Senior Customs Broker and PepsiCo Regional Compliance Lead are engaged within 4 hours.

### 7.3 Port Congestion / Vessel Delay

- **Action:** Freight Forwarder issues a delay notice within 24 hours of detecting delay.
- **Resolution:** Activate alternative port (e.g., switch from Khorfakkan to Fujairah) if delay exceeds 5 days.
- **Communication:** PepsiCo KSA Demand Planning is informed to adjust replenishment schedule.

### 7.4 Cargo Damage or Discrepancy

- **Action:** Warehouse team completes a Discrepancy Report (Form: PEP-LOG-DR-001) within 24 hours of GR.
- **Resolution:** Photos taken, surveyor engaged if damage exceeds USD 1,000 equivalent. Insurance claim initiated within 5 business days.

### 7.5 Road Accident / Truck Breakdown

- **Action:** Driver contacts Transport Provider dispatch immediately. Transport Provider notifies PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager within 1 hour.
- **Resolution:** Replacement truck dispatched; authorities notified if customs-sealed container is involved.

## 8. KPIs and Monitoring

| KPI | Target | Measurement Frequency |
|-----|--------|----------------------|
| On-Time Port Discharge | ≥ 95% within 24h of vessel berthing | Monthly |
| Border Customs Clearance Time | ≤ 48 hours | Per shipment |
| Documentation Accuracy Rate | ≥ 98% first-time correct | Monthly |
| On-Time Delivery (Port to Warehouse) | ≥ 92% on schedule | Monthly |
| Customs Hold Rate | ≤ 2% of shipments | Monthly |
| Cargo Damage/Discrepancy Rate | ≤ 0.5% of shipments | Monthly |
| Cost per TEU (Port to Warehouse) | Within ±5% of agreed budget | Monthly |
| Supplier Document Submission (on time) | ≥ 95% | Monthly |

### Monitoring Mechanism

- **Weekly:** Freight Forwarder submits shipment status report to PepsiCo Supply Chain Manager
- **Monthly:** KPI review meeting between PepsiCo, 3PL/Forwarder, Customs Broker
- **Quarterly:** Full corridor performance review including cost, service, and compliance metrics
- **Annually:** SOP review and update
