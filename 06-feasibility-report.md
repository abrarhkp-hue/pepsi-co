## 6. Final Feasibility Conclusion

### Overall Feasibility Rating: **FEASIBLE — RECOMMENDED FOR IMPLEMENTATION**

The analysis confirms that all three port corridors — Sohar (Oman), Khorfakkan (UAE), and Fujairah (UAE) — are fully feasible for PepsiCo's transborder shipment requirements into Saudi Arabia. The following conclusions are drawn:

**1. Operational Feasibility: HIGH**
All three ports have sufficient infrastructure, capacity, and regulatory frameworks to support PepsiCo's volumes. Road connectivity, customs integration, and carrier availability are adequate across all corridors.

**2. Compliance Feasibility: HIGH**
Saudi Arabia's customs framework (ZATCA/Fasah) is well-established. Key compliance requirements (Halal, SFDA, SASO, CoO) are manageable with proper pre-shipment planning. Risk is primarily in documentation management, which is controllable.

**3. Commercial Feasibility: MEDIUM–HIGH**
Cost estimates are within typical FMCG logistics benchmarks for the region. Sohar offers the most competitive total corridor cost for eastern KSA destinations. Savings of 10–15% over UAE-based corridors are achievable for Dammam-bound shipments.

**4. Risk Feasibility: MEDIUM**
Primary risks are customs-related (documentation and border clearance delays), which are manageable through broker engagement and pre-clearance practices. Supply chain resilience is enhanced by operating all three corridors rather than depending on a single route.
